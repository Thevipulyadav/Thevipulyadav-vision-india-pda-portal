Policy Roadmap 2025-2030

The Vision India : PDA Blueprint

A transformative, constitutional, and technological architecture designed under the vision of Shri Akhilesh Yadav to establish Neo India.

PILLAR 1 : PLAN (योजना)

Youth, Startups & Progressive Governance

The youth of India are not job seekers alone; they are job creators. Our plan establishes state-sponsored seed capital funds, incubator networks in tier-2 and tier-3 towns, and tax exemptions for early-stage social tech enterprises.

₹10,000 Crore Neo-India Startup Innovation Fund
AI & Robotics centers in public state universities
Transparent, paperless civil service examinations

Strategic Policy Highlights

Digital Inclusion 2.0 Equipping every matriculating youth with modern laptops and digital credentials to eliminate generational technological inequality.
Decentralized IT Hubs Expanding IT corridors beyond metros to Kanpur, Varanasi, Lucknow, and Agra to generate local high-wage employment.
Democratic Open Data Public dashboards ensuring total government transparency, public scrutiny of tenders, and citizen participation in budget allocations.
Constitutional Protections Safeguarding autonomous institutions, freedom of press, judicial independence, and democratic civil liberties.

Development Targets

Expressway Grid Expanding dual-use defense and economic expressways connecting agricultural belts to industrial ports.
Universal Free Healthcare Upgrading district hospitals to AIIMS standard with 100% free generic medicines, dialysis, and diagnostics.
Clean & Solar Energy Subsidized rooftop solar for 10 million households and aggressive rejuvenation of northern river basins.
Modern Mass Transit High-speed rapid metro networks in all cities with population exceeding 1 million.
PILLAR 2 : DEVELOP (विकास)

Green Infrastructure & Universal Healthcare

Development must be tangible, rapid, and sustainable. Following the gold standard set by the 302-km Agra-Lucknow Expressway built in record 23 months, we will revolutionize public infrastructure.

Zero-congestion urban corridors
Reinvigorating Dial 108 & 102 emergency fleet
Gomti-style riverfront and wetland eco-parks
PILLAR 3 : ASCENT (उत्थान - PDA)

PDA: Pichhde, Dalit, Alpasankhyak & Nari Shakti

True national ascent is impossible without social democracy. The PDA formula is the core conscience of our movement — ensuring that backward classes, Dalits, minorities, and women get their rightful share in representation, wealth, and dignity.

Nationwide Caste Census for proportional welfare
Expanding 1090 Women Powerline to national scale
Elimination of privatization in reservation sectors

Social Justice Guarantees

Caste Census (जातीय जनगणना) Scientific census to reveal real socio-economic inequalities and formulate equitable welfare quotas.
Women Autonomy & Safety 33% representation in local governance, financial autonomy pensions, and tech-driven fast-response legal cells.
Minority Rights & Protection Protection of cultural institutions, quality modern scholarship programs, and fair business credit access.
Constitution as Supreme Shield Defending Dr. B.R. Ambedkar’s Constitution against attempts to dilute affirmative action and federalism.
Kisan Samriddhi

Legal Guarantee for MSP & Farmer Dignity

Farmers feed the nation. Vision India commits to a legally enforceable Minimum Support Price (MSP) calculated on the Swaminathan Commission C2+50% formula, interest-free crop loans for smallholders, and modern cold storage chains.
