Official Biographical Profile

Shri Akhilesh Yadav

श्री अखिलेश यादव

20th Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh (2012–2017) • National President, Samajwadi Party • Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha)

Born on 1st July 1973 in Saifai (Etawah), Shri Akhilesh Yadav has emerged as one of modern India’s most influential progressive leaders. Combining an engineering acumen with grassroots democratic socialism, he spearheaded Uttar Pradesh's modern infrastructure revolution and pioneered the historic PDA (Pichhde, Dalit, Alpasankhyak) social justice alliance.

The PDA Vision: Social Justice as Constitutional Dharma

It is not merely an electoral equation, but an affirmative social coalition dedicated to rectifying centuries of inequality.
